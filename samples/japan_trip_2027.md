# Japan Trip Plan (March 2027)

## Dates and flights
- Depart Kuala Lumpur (KLIA) on 18 March 2027, flight MH70 at 23:30, arrive Osaka Kansai 07:05.
- Return from Tokyo Haneda on 29 March 2027, flight MH89 at 10:30.

## Itinerary
- 19-22 March: Osaka. Stay at Hotel Cross Osaka in Namba. Day trip to Nara on 21 March.
- 22-25 March: Kyoto. Ryokan near Gion. Visit Fushimi Inari early (before 8 am) to avoid crowds.
- 25-29 March: Tokyo. Apartment in Shibuya. TeamLab Planets booked for 27 March, 15:00.

## Budget
- Total budget: RM 12,000 for two people.
- JR Pass is no longer worth it for this route; use ICOCA card and a one-way Shinkansen
  ticket Kyoto to Tokyo (about JPY 14,000 each).

## Packing notes
- Cherry blossom season: bring a light jacket, evenings are around 8 degrees Celsius.
- Bring a Type A plug adapter.
